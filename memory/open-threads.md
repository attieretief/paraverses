## Commission: the first Movement 3 song — Psalm 73:2–14 (identified 2026-08-13)

All seven completed Paraverses (001–007) sit in **Movement 1, "The God Who Is"**. **Movement 3, "The
Fracture — fall, exile, and honest lament", has nothing written**, and that absence has now bitten in
practice: preparing RCBE's 16 Aug service on **Psalm 73** ("Surely God is good to those who are pure in
heart"), neither the congregation's existing canon **nor Paraverses** could carry the psalm's middle —
the envy of the wicked, the near-slip, the "brute beast" confession — so it falls entirely to the sermon.

**Psalm 73:2–14 → v17 is a textbook Movement 3 text** and the most concrete first commission available:
envy of the prospering wicked, feet almost slipping, the sanctuary as the hinge where the view changes,
and the confession of having been senseless before God. Writing it would close a hole that the
congregation's whole song list currently has — the strongest possible argument for a new song, since it
does something nothing they own can do.

**Related, from the same exercise:** the canon has essentially **no lament or doubt song at all**, so
this isn't a Paraverses gap alone — it's a gap in what that congregation can sing.
